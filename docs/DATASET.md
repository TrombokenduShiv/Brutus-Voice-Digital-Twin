# Dataset

Enrollment audio must be consented and accompanied by transcripts where possible. Preserve raw and normalized representations separately during research; raw references capture breath/performance cues while normalized audio supports timbre/acoustic analysis.

Recommended target-speaker coverage includes neutral speech, questions, commands, fast/slow delivery, emotion, numbers, names, code-switching, regional phonemes, breaths and sentence-ending styles.

Split train/validation/test by recording session, day and sentence to prevent leakage.
